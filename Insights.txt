## Business Insights and Recommedations
##DUKA SMART ELECTRONICS

**Product Perfomance  Perfomance *

Mobile Phones is the strongest generating the highest revenue of Ksh 6,942,060.65 and the highest profit of Ksh 728,760.65 
followed closely by computing at Ksh 6,596,221.7 with profit of Ksh 700,021.7.
Accessories is the weakest generating a revenue of Ksh 1,077,496.1 and achieved a profit of Ksh 456,746.1
 rpresenting an approximate profit margin of 42.4%.

**The results reveal that high revenue does not necessarily translate into the highest profitability.
 Although Mobile Phones lead in total revenue and profit, Accessories deliver a much higher profit margin.
This suggests that Accessories contribute efficiently to profitability despite their smaller sales volume.

**Business Recommendations

-Maintain sufficient inventory of Mobile Phones and Computing because they are the leading contributors to overall revenue and profit.
-Consider expanding the Accessories product range and promoting complementary purchases to increase sales.
-Review pricing, supplier costs and discount strategies across product categories to protect profit margins.
-Monitor both revenue and profit margins when making inventory and marketing investment decisions.

**Sales Perfomance by city**

**Nairobi recorded the highest sales revenue at Ksh 9,581,980.35, significantly outperforming all other cities.
 Mombasa ranked second with Ksh 3,161,889.60, while Machakos recorded the lowest revenue at Ksh 745,099.90.
 Nairobi's revenue was approximately three times that of Mombasa, highlighting a considerable difference
 in sales performance across locations.

 **The results indicate that revenue is heavily concentrated in Nairobi, suggesting that it is the business's strongest market. 
 However, the substantial revenue differences across cities may expose the business to geographical market concentration risks. 
 Lower-performing cities may also represent untapped growth opportunities.

 **Recommendations
- Maintain strong inventory availability and customer service in Nairobi to protect its leading market position.
-Investigate the factors driving Nairobi's strong performance and identify strategies that can be replicated in other cities.
-Develop targeted marketing campaigns and promotional offers in lower-performing markets, particularly Machakos.
-Evaluate customer demand, product availability and purchasing patterns across cities before expanding operational investment.

**Revenue Contribution by Customer Segment**

**VIP customers generate the highest revenue at Ksh 253,251.88, followed by Corporate customers at Ksh 197,958.71.
 Retail customers contributed the lowest revenue at Ksh 163,575.21. 
 This shows that VIP customers are the most valuable segment in terms of total revenue.

 **The results suggest that VIP customers make a significant contribution to the company's revenue. 
 Understanding the purchasing behaviour of each segment can help management develop better customer retention and marketing strategies.

 **Recommendations
 -Strengthen VIP customer retention through loyalty rewards and exclusive offers.
 -Offer Corporate customers tailored product packages and volume discounts.
 -Monitor revenue contribution by segment to identify changes in customer purchasing behaviour.

 **Customer Retention**

 **47 customers latest completed purchase was more than 90 days before the latest order date. 
 Agnes Kimani had the longest inactivity period at 625 days, followed by Purity Mwangi at 481 days and Alex Chebet at 479 days.

 **The business may be experiencing customer retention challenges. 
 If inactive customers do not return, the business could lose repeat sales and future revenue.

 **Recommendations
 -Reach out to inactive customers to understand their reasons for not returning.
-Offer special discounts to encourage repeat purchases.
-Introduce loyalty rewards to retain existing customers.
